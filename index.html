Spotify Code → Bar Heights

Extracts the 23 bar heights from the official Spotify code image.
Paste the heights into spotify_code.scad to 3D print your plaque.

How to get your Spotify link:
In Spotify: right-click any track, album, playlist or artist → Share → Copy link

Works for: tracks · albums · playlists · artists · podcasts
Paste this into spotify_code.scad

Algorithm: logo diameter = tallest bar height (reference). Each bar's height ÷ logo diameter × 8, floored, − 1 → octal 0–7.
Method by Peter Boone · boonepeter.github.io/posts/2020-11-10-spotify-codes
